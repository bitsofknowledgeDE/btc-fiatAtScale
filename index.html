Fiat at Scale

fiatAtScale visualizes US dollar money-supply expansion against Bitcoin's fixed 21 million cap. It combines live issuance estimates, historical data, inflation metrics, and future projections to show how the two monetary systems diverge.

  • Tracks estimated USD M2 creation and BTC mined since the page was opened, including their BTC-equivalent ratio.
  • Charts US dollar M2 from 1960 through 2050 alongside Bitcoin's circulating supply and 21 million maximum.
  • Compares dollar creation with Bitcoin mining at second, daily, and yearly scales.
  • Plots Truflation CPI, Bitcoin circulation growth, and Bitcoin's USD price across time.
  • Shows Bitcoin halving dates, block rewards, supply at each halving, and annual issuance rates.
  • Projects USD M2 to $85 trillion by 2050 while Bitcoin approaches its fixed 21 million supply.
